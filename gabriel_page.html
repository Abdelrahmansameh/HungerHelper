Gabriel

Where the certainties of most were swept away by the End, Gabriel’s own conviction shone ever brighter. A dispenser of unblinking justice, he served in Napoleon’s army as a Cuirassier - the elite heavy cavalry bound by the “Noblesse Oblige”. A terrifying presence on the battlefield and in the ruins of the world after the End, Gabriel’s knowledge as an elite soldier makes him a formidable foe in combat.

The cuirass began as a relic, stripped from an older age-gothic plates scavenged from a crumbling armoury, their gilding dulled by centuries. Gabriel had them reforged and set against his own Napoleonic harness, binding antiquity to the present. It is part relic, part campaign steel: a suit as old as the oath that keeps him in it.
