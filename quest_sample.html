StoryDialogue

Story

After managing to make it inside the Chateau on this Godforsaken island, I met Eloise - the young woman by the main gate. She's advised me to start introducing myself to the others here in this place known as the Outer Ramparts.

The first person I must talk to is a child named Louis. Inexplicably, it seems he is in charge of organising the Stash for those who go back to the mainland on Expeditions.

Afterwards, I must speak with a Leper named Piro. He has a number of items for sale that will come in handy.

Finally, I should talk to Reynauld - up at the top of the hill behind Louis' camp. He'll organise an Expedition to the mainland when I'm ready to go.

Dialogue

Shown in full, by phase

Before the quest 22

EloiseWelcome to the Chateau at the End of the Earth, stranger. The last refuge west of Orleans. Forgive me - it has been some time since anyone crossed the Tide. I am Eloise.

YouI haven’t seen so many Living in a long time. Are you sure this is safe?

EloiseSafer than the mainland. The Chateau shelters all sorts. After the End, people fled across the water to the one place that looked defensible. Nobles, soldiers, physicians, merchants. Even the poor traded what they had for boats, or paid guides to cross at low tide.

YouWhy are people out here?

EloiseThe cast-offs are kept here on the Outer Ramparts until they prove themselves fit to cross into the Cauldron.

YouThe Cauldron?

EloiseYes, across the Bridge. The Cauldron is the industrial heart. Beyond it lie the Galleries - the merchant quarter, the market, the auction house. Further still are the Heavenly Reaches. But none from the Outer Ramparts have ever had leave to enter there.

YouHow do we cross the Bridge?

EloiseThe longer you survive here, and the more useful you make yourself, the more you are noticed by those of higher station. On occasion they summon one of us across. It is a bitter thing - that the old order was among the first to return.

YouI see…

EloiseIf you mean to survive, introduce yourself. We look rough, but we do look after our own. Speak first to Louis. He is just across the way. If he trusts you, he will keep your possessions.

YouWhat about the giant?

EloisePay no mind to Bear. He is gentle, so long as Louis is safe. After Louis, you will need supplies. Speak to Piro.

YouPiro?

EloiseHe was a Physician by trade - before the End. Now, he helps us here on the Rampart and has a few things for sale. He can provide you with some basic supplies if you decide to take up the task of going back to the mainland on an Expedition.

YouWhat does he sell?

EloiseMostly medicine, but he often comes across other small items that might make life easier. He’ll be upset with me for saying this, but just take care when you’re with him. He has… a condition.

YouA condition? What kind of condition?

EloisePiro is a leper. Don’t let that turn you away, though. He endures a harder life on the Ramparts than most here. Although he would never admit to it, he endures it only to help us.

YouA leper? Isn’t that dangerous?

EloiseYou’re not the first to think that. Those across the Bridge in the Cauldron believe so, but Piro has taken enough precautions to satisfy us here on the Ramparts. He wears an iron mask and more. Just make sure you do not touch him. He will be careful around you.

EloiseLastly, you should speak to Reynauld, the Expedition Master. He’s a gruff old soul, but you can rely on him. You can find him up on the hill behind Louis. Come back to me once you have met them all.

While in progress 3

YouRight, I'll go speak to them.

YouI haven't met everyone yet.

EloiseLouis is just across the way. The little chap sitting on the barrel. Just follow the plume of smoke to find Piro. Reynauld is hard to miss on his high platform.

On hand-in 11

YouI spoke to Louis, Piro and Reynauld.

EloiseLouis has a sharp tongue, does he not?

YouHe does, but he’s still a child.

EloiseHave some faith. Few here are foolish enough to test Bear. Your things will be safe. And Piro?

YouHe wasn’t the friendliest.

EloiseHis affliction does not improve his temper. Reynauld explained Expeditions?

YouHe did.

EloiseI know this is a great deal to take in, and you have only just arrived. Buy what you need from Piro. Before you leave, return to me - I will have work for you. The rewards will be modest, but the good will be real.

EloiseOne more thing - I left a few items with Louis. They are in your Stash. Consider it an investment in our survival. Take only what you need. New supplies do not come easily.

YouThank you.

EloiseDo not thank me. You are the one doing the service. When you are ready, Reynauld will organise an Expedition. May you return.

Related 25

Louis

YouYou look a little young to be here alone.

LouisAnd you look like your best days are behind you!

YouOuch.

LouisHah! You must have a thicker skin than that if you managed to survive out there. It's not often that we see a new face. My name's Louis. And I'm not here alone!

YouAre your family here with you?

LouisWell, not exactly. My father brought me here long ago. And then he disappeared. Poof! Right into thin air.

YouWhere did he go?

LouisHe went back to our village to find my mother and sister, but he’ll be back very soon! Anyway, Gardon and Eloise are my family now, and Bear of course.

YouGardon? Bear?

LouisGardon! He’s in my hat. You can see him if you look closely. And Bear is my business partner. Although he doesn’t speak much.

YouI see…

LouisTell you what. After you finish speaking to Eloise, come back to me. I manage the Stash. I’ll portion you off some space. You leave whatever you want with me when you go back to the mainland. I’ve also got a request of you. I’ll reward you handsomely so long as you can do one simple trick!

Piro

YouI take it you’re Piro.

PiroHow bright you are. And? What did you want?

YouEloise said you have wares for sale.

PiroAh Eloise. Too good to everyone. Yes, I have some things - for a price. I do not barter and I do not do acts of charity. Remember that.

Reynauld

YouYou arrange the Expeditions?

ReynauldI do. Come to me when you're ready and I'll arrange passage to wherever we're able to go.

YouHow is the destination decided?

ReynauldThe fires, the storms, the winds, the clouds and the Hordes determine our passage. I warn you - we’ll get you there and we’ll bring you back, but my men can only wait around for so long before the Horde or the weather closes.

ReynauldI provide two Extraction locations per Expedition. My men make sure the first is available as soon as can be, and the second one as soon as they’re able. Once that second one opens, they’ll wait for ten minutes.

ReynauldSome Extractors find a way to call a for a third Extraction at the very last moment - if you survive long enough, maybe you’ll learn to do so, too.

YouAnd if I miss the window of Extraction?

ReynauldThen your career as an Extractor has already lasted longer than most, and the next time we meet we shall surely put you down as we do all other Hunger. I say again, do not miss the opportunity of Extraction.

ReynauldGo and talk to Eloise and make sure you’re well outfitted. When you’re ready come back to me and I’ll chart a course. Remember, best to leave the card table early with money in your pocket than stay late and lose it all.
