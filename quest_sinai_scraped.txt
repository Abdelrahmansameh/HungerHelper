Story
Eloise believes that our survival depends on information I can collect from a variety of locations. She needs practical records that will help us run our daily lives on the Ramparts.
At Sarlat Farm, I must retrieve Rozier’s Course in Agriculture from Garnier’s Windmill, and A Catechisme of Christian Doctrine, 1567 from the Chapel of Weeping Virgins. The Census of 1806 can be found in the Farmhouse.
In the tower of Jacques Bridge, I must secure The Spirit of the Laws by Montesquieu. Not too far from there I should be able to find Buffon's Natural History, General and Particular in the Carriage House.
Finally, in Sombre Forest, I should be able to find a copy of the Codex Medicamentarius in the infirmary camp, and lastly, Course in Civil Architecture by Rondelet in the Black Wood Mill.
Together, these documents will help us manage resources, people and structures more effectively - giving us a chance to rebuild after The End.
Guide
Course in Agriculture - Rozier: Garnier's Windmill (Sarlat Farm), on a desk
Census of 1806: Farmhouse (Sarlat Farm), on the ledger desk in the second room
Catechisme of Christian Doctrine, 1567: Chapel of Weeping Virgins, on the altar
Natural History, General and Particular - Buffon: Carriage House (Jacques Bridge), on the fireplace board
The Spirit of the Laws - Montesquieu: Tower (Jacques Bridge), on the top desk
Codex Medicamentarius: Infirmary Camp (Sombre Forest), on a ledger desk inside a tent
Course in Civil Architecture - Rondelet: Black Wood Mill (Sombre Forest), on a box next to the upstairs ledger desk
