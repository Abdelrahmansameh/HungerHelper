Character progression

Mastery trees

5 trees share one pool of mastery points earned by levelling up. Each rank costs 1 point; a tier opens once 5 points are spent in the tier above. Only one Tier VIII mastery can be unlocked.

Did you know? You earn 91 mastery points (one per level from 10 to 100), but maxing every tree would take 378: you have to choose.
