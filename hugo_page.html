Hugo

A distinguished and loyal officer in Napoleon's army until he vanished during the disastrous retreat from Moscow. Hugo reappeared in Paris years later, a very different man. Free of his titles, rank, faith, and morals, he embraced a life at the margins of the society he once ruled. A reckless disregard for his own life mixed with a joie de vivre only achieved when hanging between life and death makes Hugo a deadly presence to friend and foe alike.
